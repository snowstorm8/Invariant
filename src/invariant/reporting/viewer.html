Invariant v0.1.0
Security property testing

Define what must never happen. Prove when it does.

Invariant explores sequences of operations across users, roles and tenants, then a deterministic evaluator decides whether an explicit security property broke. Every finding ships with a minimal, replayable counterexample.

Explore
Stateful search over multi-tenant operation sequences
02
Evaluate
Deterministic verdicts, never from status codes alone
03
Minimize
Shrink to the shortest sequence that still breaks
04
Replay
Re-run on a fresh target to confirm the finding
